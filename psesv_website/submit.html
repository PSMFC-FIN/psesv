How to submit an article

All submissions to PSESV are handled through our editorial staff individually. If you wish to submit a PSESV article, please send an e-mail to . Include author's name, E-mail address, affiliation, data source/s, and a 300 word or less abstract describing the proposed article.

If your article is accepted for publication you will be assigned to one of our editors who will work with you to ensure the visualization is formatted appropriately, the methods are complete, and the article fits within the PSESV templates.

Once the visualization and supporting documents are complete we will provide you with a link to your article on our development site. You will be asked to submit your article through your internal organization's review process for peer-reviewed articles. Once this process is complete and documented we may require additional anonymous peer-review at the descretion of our editorial staff.

Once all editorial qualifications have been completed and PSESV editorial staff are satisfied with the articles content and function it will be moved to the production site, deemed published, and assigned a permanent DOI.
