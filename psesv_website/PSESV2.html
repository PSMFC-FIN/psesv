Economic Status of the Groundfish Fisheries off Alaska Data Visualizations

Author: Ben Fissel, Alaska Fisheries Science Center

Corresponding author email: Ben.Fissel@noaa.gov
Article number: 2
Version of record online: 2 MAY 2019
DOI: 10.28966/PSESV.2018.002

Abstract
Visualization of selected ex-vessel, first-wholesale, and effort statistics from the Groundfish Economic SAFE report

Keywords
groundfish, Eastern Bering Sea, Gulf of Alaska, Aleutian Islands, seafood production, seafood harvest, fishing effort

Methods

Visualizations

  • Wholesale and ex-vessel value of the catch in the domestic commercial fisheries off Alaska
  • Effort in the groundfish fisheries off Alaska
